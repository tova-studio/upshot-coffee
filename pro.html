About Us

A boutique roastery in Heathcote Valley

Upshot Coffee is a boutique and 100% Fairtrade Organic certified roastery. We are committed to supplying you with the world’s finest Fair Trade Organic coffee.

As well as being organic, most of our coffee is grown by traditional methods by people with a legacy of working the land in a sustainable way.

These coffees, whether ‘rustic’ shade grown under forest canopy or cultivated on small farms, ripen slowly at high altitude to provide an exquisite intensity of flavour and character which makes them unique.

Upshot Coffee is committed to sustainable coffee production and Fair Trade prices for growers. We source exclusively Fair Trade origins and believe in building ongoing relationships with growers as a way to invest in the sustainability of quality organic coffee.

We roast with love and care in small batches and can ship direct from our roastery to your door.

Good to know

Your questions, answered

Is all of Upshot’s coffee Fair Trade and organic?

Yes. Upshot Coffee is a 100% Fairtrade Organic certified roastery. We source exclusively organically grown and Fair Traded coffee and are committed to Fair Trade prices for growers.

Can you deliver coffee to my door?

Yes. We ship direct from our roastery to your door, with free delivery nationwide to urban areas. Call 027 615 1039 or email us to arrange it.

Where does your coffee come from?

From seven countries. Most of it is grown by traditional methods, shade grown under forest canopy or on small farms, and ripens slowly at high altitude for an exquisite intensity of flavour.

Do you have a decaf?

Yes: Dekaff, made from natural water processed decaffeinated beans. It is sweet and smooth with gentle acidity, with citrus and caramel notes.

Can I visit the roastery in Christchurch?

Yes. The Upshot Coffee Roastery & Coffee Shop is at 131 Bridle Path Road, Heathcote Valley, Christchurch. We’re open Monday to Friday 7am–2.30pm and Saturday 8am–2.30pm.

Which blend suits my brewing method?

Jazz is a smooth espresso blend suitable for all brewing methods. If you love a richer cup, Loco is our rich, earthy, mocha-style espresso blend.
